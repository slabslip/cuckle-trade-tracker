## Transit

No DART **light rail** station on the doorstep. Oak Lawn / Lemmon has **DART bus** stops. For rail, people usually connect toward **Victory**, **Cityplace/Uptown**, or **Medical/Market Center** depending on direction — use the [DART trip planner](https://www.dart.org/) rather than memorizing a station.

## Character

- Urban residential: attached products, small lots, street parking pressure
- Mix of long-term owners and renters on the same streets
- Dining and nightlife denser toward Oak Lawn Ave, Cedar Springs, and Lemmon than on the quiet townhome interiors

## Weather and house implications (Dallas)

- HVAC runtime and filter cadence (unknown until the household records the equipment)
