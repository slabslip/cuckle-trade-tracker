# Unknowns — do not invent these

Ask the household (or look up live city/CAD tools) before treating any of these as fact.

## Occupancy

- Who lives here full-time vs visits
- Own vs rent vs family arrangement (tax-roll names are not a household roster)
- Whether there is an HOA or only city services

## Building systems

- HVAC make/model, filter size, last service
- Water heater location and age
- Electrical panel location and any 240V circuits (EV, dryer, oven)
- Garage: how many cars, opener brand, EV charger
- Elevator (some nearby Prescott townhomes have one; **unknown for 3521**)
- Roof age and last inspection
- Flood zone — check [FEMA MSC](https://msc.fema.gov/portal/home) for this lat/lng; do not assume Zone X
